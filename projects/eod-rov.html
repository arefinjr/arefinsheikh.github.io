Man-Portable EOD ROV

A field-deployable Explosive Ordnance Disposal robot built for rugged operation — every mechanical component engineered and fabricated in-house.

01

Overview

From concept to deployed prototype

This man-portable Explosive Ordnance Disposal robot was designed for compact, field-deployable bomb disposal operations. I engineered and manufactured every mechanical component in-house — from the structural carbon fiber composite frame to the custom drive system — pioneering fabrication techniques that had never been done locally in Bangladesh.

The centerpiece of the drive system is a custom globoidal worm gearbox — designed for high-torque, compact, backlash-resistant power transmission under shock and sustained loads. Off-the-shelf gearboxes couldn't fit the form factor or survive the operational demands, so I designed one from scratch, optimizing tooth geometry for load capacity and wear resistance.

The structural frame uses carbon fiber composite construction, fabricated using wet layup and vacuum bagging processes I established in-house. This approach delivered the strength-to-weight ratio needed for a man-portable platform while maintaining impact resistance under field conditions.

Technical Approach

Globoidal Worm Gearbox

Custom-designed high-torque globoidal worm gear system — optimized tooth geometry for maximum load capacity in a compact envelope, with near-zero backlash for precise manipulation tasks.

Carbon Fiber Composite Frame

Established Bangladesh's first in-house carbon fiber fabrication workflow — wet layup and vacuum bagging for structural components, achieving the strength-to-weight ratio needed for man-portable deployment.

Design for Manufacturability

Every part was designed with local manufacturing constraints in mind — CNC machined metal components and composite layups were all validated for in-house production before finalizing the design.
