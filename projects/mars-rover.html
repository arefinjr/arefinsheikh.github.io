Lead Design Engineer for an internationally ranked Mars rover chassis — 5th place globally at the Anatolian Rover Challenge 2023.

Overview

Engineered for extreme terrain

I served as Lead Design Engineer for the Mars rover competing at the Anatolian Rover Challenge in Turkey, where our team earned 5th place internationally against global university teams. I owned the complete SolidWorks structural modeling and FEA stress optimization of the rover chassis.

The chassis geometry was iteratively optimized across multiple design-analyze-refine cycles to balance competing requirements: terrain traversal capability, structural durability under extreme test conditions, payload deployment for science tasks, and aggressive weight reduction — every gram and every stress concentration accounted for.

Competition conditions included rocky terrain traversal, steep inclines, vibration, and impact loading from drops. The structural design had to survive all of these while keeping total mass within competition limits and accommodating the full electronics, sensor, and manipulation payloads.

Engineering Challenges

Technical Approach

FEA Stress Optimization

Iterative ANSYS FEA analysis across multiple load cases — impact, static payload, incline traversal, and vibration — identifying stress concentrations and removing excess material without compromising safety factors.

Weight-Optimized Structure

Aggressive topological optimization and strategic material removal reduced chassis mass while maintaining structural integrity under competition loading conditions — every gram counted toward performance.

Terrain Traversal Geometry

Suspension mounting points, wheel-well clearances, and approach angles were parametrically tuned in SolidWorks for maximum ground clearance and obstacle negotiation on Mars-analog rocky terrain.
