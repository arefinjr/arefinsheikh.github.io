Man-Portable EOD ROV

A man-portable Explosive Ordnance Disposal robot built for rugged field operation. I engineered and manufactured every mechanical component in-house, pioneering carbon fiber composite fabrication techniques for the structural frame to keep weight low while maintaining impact resistance.

The centerpiece is a custom high-torque globoidal worm gearbox — designed for compact, backlash-resistant drive power under shock and sustained loads where off-the-shelf gearboxes couldn't fit or survive.
