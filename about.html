About Me

Engineer, Builder, Problem-Solver

From a Mars rover competition in Turkey to establishing composite fabrication infrastructure in Dhaka — here's the path so far.

Bridging theory with manufacturability

I'm a mechanical engineer specializing in robotics, advanced composite fabrication, and precision mechanical design. Currently an R&D Engineer at Cybernetics Hi-Tech Solutions Ltd, I build complex mechanical hardware from concept through local manufacturing — leading structural architecture and rapid prototyping across robotics and industrial automation platforms.

I established Bangladesh's first in-house carbon fiber composite fabrication workflow and led the structural design of an internationally ranked Mars rover chassis (5th place globally, ARC '23). SolidWorks Professional (CSWP) certified, I bridge CAD design, FEA stress optimization, and composite fabrication to deliver high-performance autonomous systems.

My passion is the gap most projects fall into: the distance between a beautiful simulation and a part that survives the shop floor. I work to close it.
